> about01

I'm a Vanier Scholar and PhD candidate in Biological & Biomedical Engineering at McGill University. My research is on wearable plasmonic biosensors — small devices that read inflammatory biomarkers from sweat and interstitial fluid, non-invasively and in real time, for chronic disease management.

I don't like science that stays on the bench. As a founding engineer at Beeta Biomed and co-founder of PhenEXA, I've helped turn lab work into rapid diagnostic platforms for antimicrobial resistance and early cancer detection — writing the grants, building the prototypes, and sitting across from clinicians to find out what actually matters.

Lately I've been learning the other side of the table: as a Venture Fellow at Halo Health, Activator Lead at Nucleate Canada, and consultant at Verax Partners, evaluating early-stage healthtech and sourcing deal flow across biopharma, digital health, and devices.

# nanofabrication, surface chemistry, microfluidics & ML — turning sensing physics into clinical tools

  • Wearable plasmonic biosensors PhD · McGill

    Real-time, non-invasive monitoring of inflammatory biomarkers in sweat and interstitial fluid, targeting chronic and cardiovascular disease. The core of my Vanier-funded doctoral work.

  • Nanoplasmonic colorimetry in microfluidics Nature Nanotech.

    Accelerated colorimetric quantification of nucleic-acid biomarkers and antimicrobial susceptibility testing directly from specimens — published in Nature Nanotechnology.

  • Additive-manufactured diagnostics Master's thesis

    3D-printed microfluidic platforms for sample-to-answer colorimetric detection of pathogens, integrating additive manufacturing with nucleic-acid sensing (Lab on a Chip).
